# 📚 Kuliytik 2.0 — Your Campus Companion

Hello! I’m Mohmmed Mazen, and I built **Kuliytik 2.0** in 2025 as a next-generation mobile app for students everywhere.  
Kuliytik 2.0 is designed to help you organize your academic life—no matter which university you attend—with powerful, intuitive features and a fresh look powered by **Flutter**.

---

## 🌟 App Highlights

- **🗓️ Weekly Class Schedule:**  
  See your week at a glance and easily add, remove, or organize lectures for any university or department.

- **📚 College Library Manager:**  
  Browse, download, and automatically organize resources for each college—no manual sorting required!

- **📢 Real-time Announcements:**  
  Instantly get updates and news from your university or college.

- **🔔 Smart Reminders:**  
  Always stay ahead with notifications for deadlines and upcoming classes.

---

Kuliytik 2.0 is built for students at **any university**—not just Tripoli. It’s about making academic life simpler, smarter, and more connected.
